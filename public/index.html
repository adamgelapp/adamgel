About

ADAMgel (aqueous dietary antifreeze mix gel) is a low-cost, high-fidelity human tissue analogue. It can be stretched into thin sheets or shaped and moulded into whatever model you desire. Afterwards, it can be chopped up, reused and recycled. We offer an alternative to expensive medical simulation models.

What is ADAMgel and how is it made?

ADAMgel is a reusable tissue analogue made with psyllium fibre, water, glycol and antifoam. The mixture is heated, poured and allowed to set into sheets or moulds. Used gel can be broken down and remoulded for another teaching session.

For simulation only. Follow the guide and ingredient safety information when handling and heating the mixture.
