01 / THE MATERIAL

A simple material.
A world of applications.

Good simulation starts with the opportunity to practise.

ADAMgel stands for Aqueous Dietary fibre Antifreeze Mix gel. Developed as an economical tissue analogue, it can be shaped and layered to create models for hands-on procedural teaching.

From ultrasound-guided vascular access to more complex anatomical models, the work brings together practical model-making, clinical education and a willingness to share what works.
