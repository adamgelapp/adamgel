About

Created by Johann Willers, ADAMgel (aqueous dietary fibre antifreeze mix gel) is a low-cost, high-fidelity human tissue analogue. It can be stretched into thin sheets or shaped and moulded into whatever model you desire. Afterwards, it can be chopped up, reused and recycled. We offer an alternative to expensive medical simulation models.
